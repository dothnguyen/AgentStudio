Routing

How tickets move on its own. When a ticket reaches a status, the role named here works it in the running team, and the result decides the next status.

{{flash}}
Routing belongs to the workspace board, not to one team. Changes apply to every team that uses this board. Without routing, teams still work when you give them a task, and you can move tickets by hand.
